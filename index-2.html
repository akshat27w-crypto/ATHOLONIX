VIDEO REVIEW

01 / Upload training video

ATHLONIX runs a local pose model and object detector in your browser. It measures visible body landmarks, plots movement over time, and creates timestamped review prompts from those measurements. It does not use a cloud AI, cannot reliably identify every sport or action, and never uploads your video.

For smoother analysis, use a short clip under 200 MB with your full body visible and good lighting.

Mind performance

Focus training

Practise attention, composure and decision-making with a short mental drill.

MIND / BODY

⚡ Reaction-time drill

Tap start, wait for the signal, then tap again as quickly as possible. Stay relaxed and try to improve your own result.

READY WHEN YOU ARE

Reset between plays

1. Relax your shoulders. 2. Breathe in gently for 4 counts. 3. Breathe out slowly for 4 counts. 4. Name your next simple action. 5. Bring your attention back to play.

Repeat comfortably; never force your breathing.
